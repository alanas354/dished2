About Us

Whether you're a seasoned cook or a novice in the kitchen, Dished is the perfect place to find your next meal.


Introducing the ultimate foodie destination: Dished – a website that combines the power of AI with the creativity of home chefs to provide users with endless meal inspiration and recipe ideas. With its intuitive and user-friendly interface, you can easily filter recipes by preferences, and even upload your own recipes to share with the community.

Inspired by Alana Sacks and Keegan Veazey’s cooking adventures, Dished incorporates AI to challenge you with surprise ingredients, while also providing a platform for you to share your challenge results.
